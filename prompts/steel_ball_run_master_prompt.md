# Master Prompt — *JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Part 7: Steel Ball Run* Minecraft Mod

> **How to use this file:** paste everything below the line into a coding agent (Claude Code or similar) at the root
> of an empty folder or a fresh branch. It is written to be self-contained. The bracketed `[[…]]` values are the
> knobs you are most likely to change; everything else has a sensible default. The agent should start with
> **§11 Kickoff** and work milestone by milestone.

---

## 0. Role and goal

You are a senior Minecraft mod developer and game designer, and a JoJo fan who knows *Steel Ball Run* well. Build
**Steel Ball Run**, a story-driven NeoForge mod in which the player enters the 1890 transcontinental horse race from
San Diego to New York, learns the Zeppeli **Spin**, awakens a **Stand** through the **Saint's Corpse**, and stands
against President **Funny Valentine** and his assassins on the way to the finish line.

It should feel like *playing the manga*, not like a stand-arrows mod with a cowboy hat. The race is the spine:
every system — horses, Spin, Stands, the Corpse, the bosses — exists to make the ride from the Pacific to the
Atlantic tense, mechanical and memorable.

## 1. Tech stack and project conventions (non-negotiable)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Loader | **NeoForge 21.1.x** (ModDevGradle `net.neoforged.moddev`) |
| Minecraft | **1.21.1** |

| `mod_id` | `[[steel_ball_run]]` |
| Group / package | `[[com.steelballrun]]` |
| Display name | `JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Steel Ball Run` |
| License line | Fan-made, non-commercial. *JoJo's Bizarre Adventure* is © Hirohiko Araki / Shueisha. All assets are original or procedurally generated. |

Conventions:

* **Package by feature**: `race`, `horse`, `spin`, `stand`, `stand.tusk`, `stand.d4c`, `corpse`, `story`,
  `npc`, `landmark`, `client`, `network`, `config`, `datagen`, `gametest`, `command`, `util`.
* **Data attachments** (`AttachmentType`) for per-player state (Spin level, Stand, Act, corpse parts, race
  registration). **`SavedData`** for world state (race clock, standings, stage, corpse locations, landmarks).
  Every attachment must survive death where canon says it should (`copyOnDeath`) and sync to the client with
  a custom payload.
* **Networking** with `CustomPacketPayload` + `StreamCodec`; never trust the client for damage, cooldowns or
  race position.
* **All player-visible text in datagen lang** (`LanguageProvider`), so names and lines can be edited without code.
  No hard-coded English in Java.
* **Three configs**: client (HUD position, screen effects intensity), common, server (race scale, rules,
  difficulty, toggles). Every number in this document marked *(config)* lives in the server config.
* **GameTests** for every rule that can be checked headless (race placement math, rule violations, Spin damage
  scaling, Mandom rewind, 20th Century Boy immunity, corpse fusion, Love Train redirection).
* **Textures** are procedurally generated by a Python script in `tools/textures/` (PIL) and committed as PNGs.
  No ripped art, no ripped audio, no verbatim manga dialogue beyond names, Stand names and short attack calls.
* Keep ticking code cheap: nothing that scans all loaded entities every tick without a spatial bound.
* Build with `./gradlew build`. **If the toolchain can't be downloaded, say so plainly** — never claim a build
  succeeded or that something was tested in-game when it wasn't.

## 2. Design pillars

1. **The race is always running.** A world clock, live standings and NPC rivals who keep riding whether or not
   you are watching.
2. **Your horse is half your power.** Spin's strongest forms come from a galloping horse. Neglect it and you lose.
3. **Rotation is a skill, not a cooldown.** Spin and Tusk reward timing, aim and movement.
4. **Stands come from the Corpse.** No arrows. Stands awaken through the Devil's Palm and the Saint's Corpse.
5. **Every boss is a puzzle with a rule.** Each Stand user has a gimmick you must read and counter
   (stand still against dinosaurs, move against 20th Century Boy, stay dry against Catch the Rainbow…).
6. **Canon first, gameplay second, never boring.** Where the manga is vague or unfit for a block game, choose the
   most fun interpretation and note it in the README.

## 3. The Race

### 3.1 Registration and start
* A **Race Office** at **San Diego Beach** (spawn-adjacent landmark) with **Stephen Steel** and a grandstand.
  Right-click Steel to register: pick a **racer background** (§3.5), receive a **Race Number bib** (armor
  trim-style chest overlay), a **Race Rulebook** (written book) and a **Map of America** item that shows stage
  checkpoints.
* The race starts at a fixed world time *(config: `startDay`, default day 3)*, or by `/sbr race start`.
  Registration closes at the gun. Late joiners can register as **spectators** or **unofficial riders**
  (no prize, still eligible for the Corpse story).
* The start is a scripted moment: NPC racers line up, Steel fires the gun, crowd cheers (particles, sounds).

### 3.2 The route (stages)
The route runs along one world axis from San Diego (start, near spawn) to New York (finish). Distances are scaled
by *(config) `routeScale`*, default **one block = 0.5 km → ≈12,000 blocks** total. Each stage ends at a
**checkpoint gate** (landmark). Loosely following the manga:

| # | Stage | Terrain theme | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | San Diego Beach sprint | beach → scrub | the start; first rivals (Gyro, Diego, Sandman) |
| 2 | Arizona Desert | desert, cacti, mesas | the Devil's Palm; first corpse part; Scary Monsters user |
| 3 | Monument Valley | red mesas, canyons | Mountain Tim; Oyecomova; Boom Boom family |
| 4 | Rocky Mountains | peaks, snow, pines | Sugar Mountain's spring; Ringo Roadagain |
| 5 | Great Plains / Kansas | grassland, rain storms | Blackmore (rain); Pork Pie Hat Kid |
| 6 | Mississippi River | river crossing, ferry | Mike O.; 20th Century Boy |
| 7 | Lake Michigan / Chicago | frozen lake, city | Wekapipo; Hot Pants alliance; Axl Ro |
| 8 | Philadelphia | city, coast, Navy Yard | **Funny Valentine — D4C → Love Train** |
| 9 | New York | Manhattan, final dash | finish at the church; the alternate Diego (epilogue) |

Treat this table as the default script; keep stage ↔ boss assignment in data so it can be reshuffled.

### 3.3 Standings, points and the prize
* Live standings for every registered racer (players + NPC rivals). Stage placement awards points
  *(config table, default 1st 100 · 2nd 50 · 3rd 30 · then descending)*. Overall winner = most points at the end.
* **NPC rivals don't need to be loaded.** Each NPC racer has a *virtual position* along the route advanced by a
  simulated speed (horse stats + noise + events). When a player comes within render range, the NPC is spawned
  at its virtual position and rides for real; when out of range, it despawns back to simulation. This is the
  single most important technical decision in the mod — get it right before anything else.
* The prize is **$50,000,000** of in-game **Dollars** (item currency, also used at trading posts along the route).
  Real reward: a trophy block, a statue of you at the finish, and an achievement.

### 3.4 Race rules (enforced)
*(each a config toggle; violation = time penalty or disqualification by a race official)*
* Must cross every checkpoint gate in order; skipping one = disqualification.
* No **elytra**, **ender pearls**, **chorus fruit**, **nether-portal shortcuts**, **minecarts** or **boats**
  (except the official Mississippi ferry). Teleporting Stand abilities are canonically allowed but get logged.
* You may run on foot (the Sandman rule), but horses are expected.
* Changing horses mid-race is allowed only at official stations, for a points penalty.

### 3.5 Racer backgrounds (chosen at registration)
Small passive perks, nothing that replaces progression:
* **Jockey** — horse stamina +15 %, better jump timing window. (Johnny-flavoured; optional *Paralysed Legs*
  hard mode: you can't walk unmounted until Spin heals you partially.)
* **Zeppeli Apprentice** — starts with 2 steel balls and Spin level 1.
* **Cowboy** — starts with a lasso and revolver; better at roping horses.
* **Native Runner** — no horse needed; sprint stamina scaling that keeps up with a horse on flat ground.
* **Wanderer** — random starting horse breed and $500.

## 4. Horses

* Replace nothing in vanilla; add a **Racehorse** entity (extends `AbstractHorse`) with hidden stats:
  **Top Speed, Acceleration, Stamina, Endurance (long-distance recovery), Jump, Terrain Affinity**
  (desert / mountain / plains / snow), and **Bond**.
* **Stamina bar** in the HUD while mounted. Galloping drains it, trotting regenerates slowly, resting
  regenerates fast. Exhaustion = forced walk + the horse can be injured.
* **Care**: feeding (hay, apples, sugar, carrots — different effects), water, brushing (item), shoeing at
  blacksmiths (better terrain grip). Bond rises with care and riding; high Bond unlocks the **Golden Spin
  from horse power** (§5.3).
* **Named canon horses** (unique, one per world, ridden by their NPC owners; can't be stolen):
  *Slow Dancer* (Johnny), *Valkyrie* (Gyro), *Silver Bullet* (Diego), plus horses for the other rivals.
  Players buy or tame their own from **horse traders** along the route.
* Saddlebags (extra inventory), horseshoes (tiered), a **racing saddle** (+speed, −armor).
* Horse breeds with distinct coat textures: Appaloosa, Quarter Horse, Thoroughbred, Mustang, Arabian.

## 5. Spin (Zeppeli technique)

### 5.1 Steel balls
* **Steel Ball** item. Hold use to wind up, release to throw; the ball **returns to your hand** like a bound
  trident, keeping its spin. Up to 2 balls equipped at once.
* Hit effects scale with Spin level and charge: knockback with vibration, **muscle manipulation**
  (target's limbs lock: slowness + mining fatigue + can't use items for a moment), skin-ripple hit particles.
* Ball variants via smithing later: Wrecking Ball steel balls (§7) and Ball Breaker balls (§5.4).

### 5.2 Spin techniques (unlock by level)
1. **Spin Throw** (above).
2. **Spin Scan** — a ball on the ground sends vibrations: highlights hidden entities and Stand users nearby.
3. **Spin Healing** — use on an ally or yourself to set bones: heals and clears slowness/weakness,
   costs hunger.
4. **Spin Shield** — rotating ball blocks projectiles for a short window.
5. **Golden Rectangle Spin** — perfect-timing throw (§5.3).

### 5.3 The Golden Rectangle (the signature mechanic)
* While charging, a **spiral HUD overlay** appears with a sweet-spot ring that tightens on the golden ratio.
  Releasing in the window = **Golden Spin**: much more damage, pierces armor, ignores knockback resistance.
* **Learning it is a quest**: study the ratio in nature. Use a **Sketchbook** on sunflowers, ferns, nautilus
  shells, tree leaves and mesa layers; each unique find shrinks the difficulty of the timing window.
* **Horse-powered Golden Spin**: throwing while galloping at full speed with high Bond widens the window and
  multiplies the result. This is what powers **Ball Breaker** and **Tusk Act 4**.

### 5.4 Ball Breaker (late game)
Unlocked after the Valentine arc's midpoint: a Golden Spin thrown from a galloping horse summons **Ball
Breaker**, a Stand-like projection that rips through dimensions and **ages** what it touches
(withering + max-health reduction while it lasts). It is the only thing besides Tusk Act 4 that can harm
Valentine under Love Train.

## 6. Stands and the Saint's Corpse

### 6.1 The Saint's Corpse
* **Nine parts** hidden along the route: **left arm, eyes, heart, spine, ears, right arm, legs, torso, head**.
  Each sits in a **Devil's Palm** event or a boss drop. Locations are seeded per world and stored in `SavedData`.
* **Picking up a part** fuses it with you (it becomes part of your body, not an inventory item): the first part
  you carry **awakens your Stand** if you don't have one, and each part grants a passive
  (e.g. eyes → see Stand users through walls briefly; heart → regeneration; legs → horse speed).
* **Valentine's agents hunt part holders.** Carrying parts makes you a target: assassin encounters scale with the
  number of parts you hold.
* Parts can be taken by defeating the holder, which is how the Valentine arc pressures you.
* With all nine parts gathered by anyone, the finale triggers.

### 6.2 The Devil's Palm
Random world events along the route: a patch of land where the ground pulls inward, compasses spin, and
a corpse part (or a lesser "relic") lies at the center. Walking in awakens latent Stand users (both players and
some NPCs) — this is the canonical origin of most Stands in the part.

### 6.3 Stand system
* A Stand is summoned with a key *(default `G`)*; it renders as a translucent humanoid (or non-humanoid) that
  follows and attacks. **Five ability slots** (`Z X C V B`) plus a **Stand menu** (`K`) like the house style.
* **Stand stats** (Power, Speed, Range, Durability, Precision, Potential — A to E) shown in the menu and
  affecting numbers.
* **Stand energy/stamina**: abilities spend Stand stamina; damage to the Stand is shared with the user.
* Only Stand users see Stands *(config)*.

### 6.4 Player Stands (awakened from the Corpse — weighted random, or chosen by background)

| Stand | Canon user | In-game kit |
|---|---|---|
| **Tusk** (Act 1–4) | Johnny Joestar | §6.5 — the flagship Stand |
| **Scary Monsters** | Diego Brando / Dr. Ferdinand | transform into a raptor: speed, sharp senses (moving entities glow); bites spread **dinosaurization** (infected mobs become small dinos that fight for you); weakness: cold |
| **Cream Starter** | Hot Pants | flesh spray: heal/seal wounds on self and allies, disguise as another player's skin, suffocating spray on enemies |
| **In a Silent Way** | Sandman | onomatopoeia as solid sound: throw a word that detonates; plant sound traps on blocks; super-sprint on foot |
| **Oh! Lonesome Me** | Mountain Tim | a lasso; split your body along the rope to dodge, reassemble; tie enemies and drag them apart |
| **Hey Ya!** | Pocoloco | luck: higher crits, better loot, a whisper that points to the best route or nearest corpse part |
| **Mandom** | Ringo Roadagain | **rewind 6 seconds**: positions, health and projectiles of all entities within 32 blocks snap back to a snapshot; only you remember (your cooldowns persist). Requires a pocket watch |
| **Catch the Rainbow** | Blackmore | only in rain: freeze raindrops to walk on air, turn rain into blades, scatter your body into the rain to dodge |
| **20th Century Boy** | Magent Magent | invulnerable while you stand completely still (hold key); damage taken is grounded into the earth; can't act while active |
| **Tubular Bells** | Mike O. | turn iron nuggets into balloon animals that hunt targets and pop into steel spikes inside them |
| **Wired** | Pork Pie Hat Kid | drop fishhooks from the sky on a marked target and reel them |
| **Tomb of the Boom** | Boom Boom family | magnetism: pull iron items/armor, iron-sand clone decoys, magnetized-body lightning-rod trick |
| **Civil War** | Axl Ro | the things a target has dropped or discarded come back for them (track items each player tossed) |
| **The Spring** (event, not a Stand you keep) | Sugar Mountain | a tree that exchanges what you give it for riches — **you must spend it all before sunset** or be trapped in the tree |

D4C, Love Train, Ticket to Ride and The World (alternate Diego) are **boss-only** by default *(config toggle
`allowBossStandsForPlayers`)*.

### 6.5 Tusk (flagship — get it right)
* **Act 1 — Nail Bullets**: fire your ten fingernails as spinning bullets (10 ammo, nails regrow over time).
* **Act 2 — Spinning Holes**: a nail hit leaves a **hole** that crawls toward the target and keeps tracking.
  Requires Spin level 3 and a corpse part.
* **Act 3 — Wormhole**: shoot yourself to dive into your own hole and emerge from any hole you've planted —
  short-range teleport through walls, and point-blank nail shots from inside. Requires 4 parts.
* **Act 4 — Infinite Rotation**: only usable mounted at full gallop with a Golden Spin. A hit locks the target in
  **infinite rotation**: rooted, taking repeated damage that ignores regeneration, totems and dimension-hopping,
  until death or the user releases it. Bypasses Love Train. Boss balance: bosses get a "break free" timer.
* Act unlocks are milestones in the story, announced with a title card.

## 7. NPC rivals and Valentine's assassins

Rivals (racers with full AI and a virtual race position):
**Gyro Zeppeli** (Spin, Valkyrie — friendly; can become your partner after the Arizona arc), **Johnny Joestar**
(if you aren't playing Johnny-flavoured), **Diego Brando** (Scary Monsters, Silver Bullet — rival, sometimes ally),
**Sandman**, **Pocoloco**, **Mountain Tim**, **Hot Pants**, and a field of generic racers with random horses.

Assassins and bosses — each a **puzzle fight** with a boss bar and a readable rule:

| Boss | Rule you must learn |
|---|---|
| **Dr. Ferdinand** (Scary Monsters) | dinosaurs only see movement — stand still to vanish; cold slows them |
| **Oyecomova** | his pins/bombs are on everything he touched — break contact, fight at range |
| **Boom Boom family** (3 fights) | drop iron armor or become a magnet; lure them into lightning |
| **Sandman** (if he turns on you) | sounds are solid — stay quiet (sneaking muffles his traps) |
| **Ringo Roadagain** | Mandom loops 6 seconds; you keep the knowledge — win inside one loop (a "walk the path" duel) |
| **Blackmore** | only works in rain: drag him under a roof, or wait for the storm to break |
| **Mike O.** | balloon animals home in on you — pop them at range first |
| **Magent Magent** | invulnerable while still — make him move (water, knockback into the river) |
| **Wekapipo** | **Wrecking Ball**: satellite balls cause **left-side sensory loss** (left half of screen darkens, inputs drift right). Later becomes an ally |
| **Axl Ro** | Civil War pulls your discarded items; don't throw things away during the fight |
| **Funny Valentine** | §8 |
| **Alternate Diego** (*The World*) | time stop: watch his tells; epilogue boss at the finish |

## 8. Funny Valentine — the finale

### 8.1 D4C (Dirty Deeds Done Dirt Cheap)
* **Sandwich travel**: when he is between two objects (a flag and the ground, two walls, a door and its frame) he
  steps into a **parallel world** — he vanishes and reappears elsewhere, possibly healed (swapped with an
  uninjured alternate).
* **Alternates**: he summons parallel-world copies of himself, and of *you*. If you **touch your own alternate**,
  both of you start breaking into a **Menger-sponge cube** pattern (rapid damage + cube particles) — separate fast.
* **Drag-along**: he can pull the player into a parallel world (a short instanced copy of the arena) and leave
  you there unless you find a sandwiched gap to escape.
* Phase transition: when he has gathered every corpse part he holds, he activates **Love Train**.

### 8.2 Love Train
* A wall of light surrounds him. Damage aimed at him is **redirected as misfortune** to another entity somewhere
  else in the world (pick a random entity within a large radius; show it on the HUD).
* Only **Tusk Act 4** or **Ball Breaker** pierce it. If you have neither, the fight is unwinnable — the game
  should telegraph this long before Philadelphia.
* His **offer**: mid-fight he proposes a deal (clickable chat): give up your corpse parts and he sends you home
  to a world where your wish is granted. Accepting = a bad ending; refusing continues the fight.

### 8.3 Endings
* **Win the race and stop Valentine** → true ending, statue, trophy.
* **Stop Valentine but lose the race** → bittersweet ending.
* **Accept Valentine's deal** → bad ending: he keeps the corpse, the race finishes without you.
* **Epilogue**: the alternate Diego arrives with **The World** at the finish line. Optional final fight.

## 9. World, landmarks and atmosphere

* Landmarks placed from the seed along the route, built **chunk by chunk as players approach** (≈170 blocks)
  so they appear in existing worlds; ground flattened under each footprint. `/sbr atlas` lists them.
* Required landmarks: **San Diego Beach** (start line, grandstand, Race Office), **Arizona stage camp**,
  **Monument Valley mesas**, **Devil's Palm** sites (event-based), **Rocky Mountain pass** with **Sugar
  Mountain's spring**, **Ringo's cabin**, **Kansas plains town**, **Mississippi ferry crossing**, **Lake
  Michigan frozen shore**, **Chicago skyline**, **Philadelphia** (Independence-era buildings, harbor),
  **New York** (Manhattan streets and the finish line at the church), plus small **waystation towns** every
  ≈600 blocks (horse trader, blacksmith, saloon, inn, telegraph office, sheriff).
* **Terrain theming**: each stage landmark paints its surroundings (sand/terracotta for the desert, snow for the
  Rockies, grass for Kansas) within a radius. Stretch goal: an optional world preset *"America 1890"* whose
  biome source lays biomes in bands along the route.
* **Period flavor**: 1890 — revolvers and rifles (simple ranged items), lassos, top hats, stetsons, ponchos,
  telegrams (in-game mail between players), newspapers that report the standings each morning.
* **Music & sound**: original sounds only (galloping hoof loops, steel ball whirr, nail shot, Stand summon);
  no copyrighted music.

## 10. UI, commands, config

**HUD** (client config anchors): race panel (stage, your place / total, distance to next checkpoint, race clock),
horse stamina bar, Spin charge spiral, Stand stamina, five ability slots, corpse-part icons.

**Screens**: Stand menu (`K`) with stats chart and slot assignment; race standings (`Tab` while registered or
`/sbr standings`); Map of America item screen with route, checkpoints and your marker.

**Commands**
```
/sbr standings | atlas | rules                     anyone
/sbr race start | stop | reset | stage <n> | status
/sbr race skip <stage>                             jump the race forward (testing)
/sbr corpse give <player> <part> | locate <part> | reset
/sbr stand set <player> <stand> | act <player> <1-4>
/sbr spin level <player> <n>
/sbr horse summon <breed|canon_name>
/sbr boss summon <id>                              e.g. ferdinand, ringo, blackmore, valentine
/sbr landmark list | build <type> | rebuild <id> | remove <id>
```

**Server config**: `routeScale`, `startDay`, stage points table, rule toggles, NPC racer count and speed spread,
assassin frequency per corpse part, Devil's Palm frequency, `allowBossStandsForPlayers`, Stand visibility,
Mandom radius/seconds, Love Train redirect radius, landmarks enabled + chunks per tick, difficulty multipliers.

## 12. Quality bar

* Every ability: a clear telegraph (particles + sound), server-authoritative hit detection, a cooldown shown
  in the HUD, and a GameTest where testable.
* Every boss: a boss bar, a readable rule, a counter the player can discover, and no instant-kill without warning.
* No per-tick global scans, no unbounded entity lists, no client-trusted damage.
* Old worlds keep working across versions (attachment and `SavedData` migrations, never crash on missing keys).
* README for players: what to do first, the race, horses, Spin, Stands, bosses, commands, config, and a
  "canon liberties" section. Keep a CHANGELOG.
* At the end of every milestone, report honestly: what was built, what was tested and how, what **wasn't**
  tested in-game, and known issues.
